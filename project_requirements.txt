## 🎯 ACTIVE TARGET: SCENE 1 — THE CRASH (ACT I)

### Scene Narrative
The player wakes up disoriented in the snow after Flight 402 crashes deep in the sub-zero wilderness. Smoke billows from the sheared aircraft fuselage.

### Key Scene Elements
- **Environment:** Smoldering crash clearing, sheared fuselage, tilted wing with airline livery, detached jet turbine.
- **Atmosphere:** Thick rising smoke particles, flickering engine fire light, cold howling wind.
- **Interactables:**
  - Emergency ration crates (salvage food into persistent `GameState.inventory.food`)
  - First aid kit near cockpit (restore health into persistent `GameState.player.health`)
  - Damaged Emergency Beacon (inspect and discover dead battery / power requirement)
- **Objective:** Salvage supplies and inspect the emergency beacon to trigger transition to Scene 2.

## 📖 STORY STRUCTURE BREAKDOWN

### ACT I — THE CRASH
- **Scene 1 — The Crash:** Wake up after crash. Wreckage, smoke, salvage supplies, inspect dead emergency beacon.
- **Scene 2 — The Last Fire:** Beacon requires power & warmth. Gather wood, keep campfire burning, power beacon, survive 1-minute night. Sunrise arrives: "DISTRESS SIGNAL RECEIVED" — but no rescue arrives.

### ACT II — STRANDED
- **Scene 3 — Morning After:** Morning breaks; silence. Investigate wreckage deeper; discover flight documents revealing the crash was not an accident.
- **Scene 4 — Search for Survivors:** Discover footprints, blood in the snow, broken branches; track the survivor's trail into the woods.
- **Scene 5 — The Other Survivor:** Find an injured survivor; choose to help, question, or treat them. Survival becomes about people.

### ACT III — THE FOREST
- **Scene 6 — Into the Forest:** Explore beyond the clearing; rivers, caves, animal trails, strange markings.
- **Scene 7 — The Old Camp:** Stumble upon a ruined campsite; old tools, maps, journal. Proof that others were stranded here before.
- **Scene 8 — The Signal:** Power an abandoned transmitter; pick up a cryptic loop: "...repeat... do not approach... coordinates... they are still here...".

### ACT IV — THE SETTLEMENT
- **Scene 9 — Establishing Camp:** Realizing rescue isn't coming soon, establish a permanent fortified camp (shelter, storage, fire).
- **Scene 10 — More Survivors:** Additional survivors appear; manage personalities, division of labor, and ideological conflicts.
- **Scene 11 — The Storm:** A catastrophic storm tests the camp; maintaining shelter and warmth while tending to injured companions.

### ACT V — THE TRUTH
- **Scene 12 — The Facility:** Locate a hidden underground research / military communications complex.
- **Scene 13 — The Records:** Explore the archives: flight manifests, black-box telemetry, classified research dossiers.
- **Scene 14 — The Truth:** The climax of the mystery: why the plane was downed and what the facility was studying.

### ACT VI — THE ESCAPE
- **Scene 15 — Build the Way Out:** Pool resources and technical knowledge to construct an extraction beacon / repair signal tower.
- **Scene 16 — The Journey:** Multi-stage trek across the river and mountain pass toward the extraction coordinates.
- **Scene 17 — The Final Night:** Trapped at the extraction ridge; defend the camp and fire through one brutal final night.
- **Scene 18 — Rescue:** Sunrise; rescue helicopters break through the clouds; survivor reactions to extraction.
- **Scene 19 — The Ending:** Branching resolution:
  - **Ending A (Rescue):** Return to civilization.
  - **Ending B (Stay):** The wilderness is now home.
  - **Ending C (The Truth):** Leak classified evidence to the world.
  - **Ending D (Sacrifice):** One stays behind to ensure the others escape.

## ⚙️ SCENE-ISOLATION ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

1. **Modular Scene Encapsulation:**
   - Every scene lives in `src/scenes/scene-<id>/Scene<Id>.js`.
   - Each scene instantiates and owns its own `THREE.Scene`, `camera`, and `World` (ECS).
   - Leaving a scene calls `exit()` and `dispose()`, purging all geometries, materials, ECS entities, and listeners.
2. **Data-Driven Declarative Transitions:**
   - Scene ordering and allowed transitions are configured in `src/scenes/sceneConfig.js`.
   - Scene registration is maintained in `src/scenes/sceneRegistry.js`.
   - The engine supports reordering scenes by editing configuration without changing system code.
3. **Cross-Scene State Persistence:**
   - Persistent player vitals, inventory items, and narrative flags are managed exclusively by `src/core/GameState.js`.
   - Entering a scene reads from `GameState`; exiting or interacting updates `GameState`.
4. **Style Guide Compliance (`style_guide.txt`):**
   - Anti-AI, physical game aesthetic: dark earth/forest palette, restrained radii (2px–4px), clean Lucide SVG icons, zero emojis, contextual physical key prompts (`<span class="key-badge">E</span>`).
