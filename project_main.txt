CRASH CAMP — STORY STRUCTURE
============================


ACT I — THE CRASH
-----------------

SCENE 1 — THE CRASH

The player wakes up after the plane crash.

Key elements:
- Damaged aircraft
- Smoke
- Wreckage
- Basic exploration
- Basic interaction
- Emergency supplies
- Emergency beacon

Objective:
Find a way to get the emergency beacon working.


SCENE 2 — THE LAST FIRE

The player discovers that the emergency equipment needs power.

The player must:

Collect wood
    ↓
Keep the fire alive
    ↓
Keep the beacon powered
    ↓
Survive the night
    ↓
Transmit the distress signal

Ending:
SUNRISE

DISTRESS SIGNAL RECEIVED

But no rescue arrives.

This creates the first major mystery.


==================================================


ACT II — STRANDED
-----------------

SCENE 3 — MORNING AFTER

The player wakes up after the night.

The rescue never came.

The player investigates the crash site.

Discoveries:
- Emergency supplies
- Food
- Medical equipment
- Flight documents
- Aircraft damage
- Signs that something about the crash is unusual

Story realization:

THE CRASH MAY NOT HAVE BEEN AS STRAIGHTFORWARD AS IT SEEMED.


SCENE 4 — SEARCH FOR SURVIVORS

The player discovers signs that someone else survived.

Clues:
- Footprints
- Blood
- Broken branches
- Abandoned belongings

The player follows the trail.


SCENE 5 — THE OTHER SURVIVOR

The player finds another survivor.

The survivor remembers very little about the crash.

The player begins developing their first relationship.

Possible choices:
- HELP THEM
- LEAVE THEM
- QUESTION THEM

The player realizes:

SURVIVAL IS NOT JUST ABOUT RESOURCES.

IT IS ABOUT PEOPLE.


==================================================


ACT III — THE FOREST
--------------------

SCENE 6 — INTO THE FOREST

The player and survivor explore farther from the crash site.

Discoveries:
- Rivers
- Caves
- Animal trails
- Strange markings
- Abandoned equipment
- Unknown structures

The forest becomes increasingly dangerous.


SCENE 7 — THE OLD CAMP

The survivors discover evidence that someone lived here before.

Discoveries:
- Abandoned campsite
- Old tools
- Ruined shelter
- Journal
- Radio
- Maps

Major realization:

THEY ARE NOT THE FIRST PEOPLE TO BECOME STRANDED HERE.


SCENE 8 — THE SIGNAL

The survivors discover an old radio/transmitter.

It receives a strange signal.

Possible transmission:

"...repeat..."

"...do not approach..."

"...coordinates..."

"...they are still here..."

The story changes from:

HOW DO WE SURVIVE?

to:

WHERE EXACTLY ARE WE?


==================================================


ACT IV — THE SETTLEMENT
-----------------------

SCENE 9 — ESTABLISHING CAMP

The survivors realize they may be stranded for a long time.

They establish a permanent camp.

Gameplay expands into:

- Shelter
- Storage
- Fire
- Resource gathering
- Basic crafting
- Camp upgrades

The camp becomes the player's home.


SCENE 10 — MORE SURVIVORS

More survivors begin appearing.

The group grows.

Possible survivors:
- Survivor A
- Survivor B
- Survivor C
- Survivor D

They have different personalities and opinions.

Conflict begins.

One wants to stay.

One wants to leave.

One wants to explore.

One wants to conserve resources.

One doesn't trust the player.


SCENE 11 — THE STORM

A major storm hits.

Problems:
- Food becomes scarce
- Fire becomes difficult to maintain
- Shelter is damaged
- Someone becomes injured
- Resources become limited

Major realization:

KEEPING EVERYONE ALIVE IS HARDER THAN KEEPING YOURSELF ALIVE.


==================================================


ACT V — THE TRUTH
-----------------

SCENE 12 — THE FACILITY

The survivors discover an old facility hidden deep in the wilderness.

Possible identity:
- Research station
- Communications station
- Military facility
- Weather station
- Experimental facility

The facility is connected to the mysterious signal.


SCENE 13 — THE RECORDS

The player explores the facility.

Discoveries:
- Recordings
- Documents
- Photographs
- Maps
- Research
- Flight information

The player begins connecting the pieces.

Major realization:

THE CRASH WAS NOT RANDOM.


SCENE 14 — THE TRUTH

The player finally discovers what caused the crash.

Possible explanations:

- The aircraft encountered something unusual.
- Someone deliberately caused the crash.
- The location is hiding something.
- The survivors were not random passengers.
- The facility was connected to the aircraft.

The exact truth can be decided later.

The important thing is that the mystery reaches its major reveal here.


==================================================


ACT VI — THE ESCAPE
-------------------

SCENE 15 — BUILD THE WAY OUT

The survivors discover a possible way to escape.

Possible solutions:
- Repair the aircraft
- Reach an old radio tower
- Repair a boat
- Activate an emergency transmitter
- Reach an extraction zone
- Build a large-scale signal system

Everything learned and built throughout the game now becomes useful.


SCENE 16 — THE JOURNEY

The survivors leave the camp.

Possible journey:

CAMP
  ↓
FOREST
  ↓
RIVER
  ↓
MOUNTAIN
  ↓
FACILITY
  ↓
EXTRACTION POINT


This can eventually become a large exploration/procedural-world section.


SCENE 17 — THE FINAL NIGHT

The survivors are trapped.

The escape/rescue mechanism needs time.

The player must protect the group through one final night.

The first game was:

THE LAST FIRE

The final chapter becomes:

THE FINAL NIGHT

The difference:

THE LAST FIRE

Player
    ↓
Fire
    ↓
Survival


THE FINAL NIGHT

Player
    ↓
Survivors
    ↓
Camp
    ↓
Resources
    ↓
Everyone's survival


The story has come full circle.


SCENE 18 — RESCUE

Morning arrives.

The signal works.

Rescue finally arrives.

Possible presentation:

RESCUE SIGNAL CONFIRMED

SURVIVORS LOCATED

EXTRACTION IN PROGRESS


The survivors react differently.

Some are relieved.

Some don't want to leave.

Some have changed too much.


SCENE 19 — THE ENDING

The player makes a final decision.

Possible endings:

ENDING A — RESCUE

Everyone leaves.

RESCUE CONFIRMED

SURVIVORS: 7

HOME


ENDING B — STAY

The player decides the island has become home.

WE WERE STRANDED HERE.

NOW WE CHOOSE TO STAY.


ENDING C — THE TRUTH

The player exposes what caused the crash.

The outside world finally learns what happened.


ENDING D — SACRIFICE

Someone stays behind so everyone else can escape.

STORY ACTS
----------

ACT I
THE CRASH
Purpose: Introduce the player and establish survival.


ACT II
STRANDED
Purpose: Introduce other survivors and establish the mystery.


ACT III
THE FOREST
Purpose: Expand exploration and reveal that others were here before.


ACT IV
THE SETTLEMENT
Purpose: Transform survival into community and introduce relationships/conflict.


ACT V
THE TRUTH
Purpose: Reveal the mystery behind the crash.


ACT VI
THE ESCAPE
Purpose: Resolve the survival story and give the player an ending.
